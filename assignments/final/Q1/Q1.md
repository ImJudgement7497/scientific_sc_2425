It is clear from the table that ARCHER2 is focused on a CPU architecture, with 750, 080 cores in total. ARCHER2 has an estimated peak performance of 28 PFLOPs. However, Bede is focused on a GPU architecture, boasting many more high powered GPUs than ARCHER2.

- ARCHER2 is designed for general purpose high performance computing, running programs such as CASTEP and HadGEM3. The use of 64 core processors and a large amount of nodes means that ARCHER2 offers extreme benefits from parallel programming. On the other hand, Bede is desgined for more specialised applications that need GPU resources such as machine learning, a more modern demand. 

- For large parallel programs, the communication between different pieces of hardware is extremely important. ARCHER2 has a 100 Gbps interconnect which is quite fast and does help minimise communication time. However Bede's NVLink 2.0 offers a 25 Gigatransfers per second, which is an estimated 1600 Gbps. This much higher interconnect is needed for the high memory usage that GPU programs typically need.

- ARCHER2 has a focus on scalability that programs from a variety of disciplines can be ran on it, making it a central computational resource for the wider country. However, Bede has more of a focus on specialised studies such as AI, machine learning and neural networks and the decision to focus on GPUs gives researchers a great computational resource for their own studies.

For GROMACs, both supercomputers offer their own benefits to being used.
